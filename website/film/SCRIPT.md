# Dark at Dawn: narration

One voice, calm and documentary, about 150 words a minute. Each line is one cue; the picture changes when the line starts.
Numbers marked (real) come from our pipeline run on the Sentinel-1D pass of 2 Nov 2026. The tanker, its name, the oil,
buoy B-07 and its readings are a dramatized scenario, and the film says so in its first and last seconds.
