01 · The problem

About three in four industrial fishing vessels are missing from public ship tracking.

72–76%

of the world's industrial fishing vessels are not publicly tracked. Ships can stop broadcasting, and the sea is too big to patrol everywhere. What happens in that gap goes unseen.

Global figure, 2017 to 2021, not specific to the UAE. Dark does not mean illegal, and radar shows that a ship is there, not who it is. Source: Paolo et al., Nature 625, 85–91 (2024).

AIS is optional in practice

A transponder can be switched off, or report a false position. Public tracking only knows what ships say about themselves.

The sea is big

Patrol boats and aircraft see a few kilometres at a time. Without a lead, they search blind.

Harm hides in the gaps

Illegal fishing, smuggling and oil dumping are easier when nobody can see a ship is there.

04 · Our solution

Radar minus AIS
equals the dark vessels.

The rule

A ship is matched when an AIS report lies within 500 m of a radar detection. No report in range: it is flagged dark.

Why it helps

Dark is a lead, not a verdict. It tells patrols and port authorities where to look first.

In this prototype

AIS reports are simulated, because no free real Gulf feed exists. Every output is labelled that way.

One system

The satellite finds, the dashboard shows, the buoy confirms.

  1. 01 · SpaceSentinel-1 radar

    Free passes about every three days. Sees every hull, day, night and through cloud.

  2. 02 · PipelineDetect and match

    Sea mask, CFAR detector, map coordinates, then an AIS match within 500 m.

  3. 03 · DashboardSee and act

    Ships on a map, dark flags highlighted, and a ship count for each port on every pass.

  4. 04 · SeaSolar buoy

    Listens for AIS, films the surface, senses oil. Confirms or clears a flagged ship.

07 · Does the detector work?

On 1,962 radar images it never saw, it finds 82% of ships at 91% precision.

A dark flag is only as trustworthy as the detection under it. Misses hide ships, and false alarms invent them.

Precision0.91
Recall0.82
mAP500.90

YOLO11s fine-tuned on the HRSID benchmark. Scores on the held-out test split; bars run from 0 to 1.

3,278 train 364 1,962 never seen

5,604 HRSID images, split three ways (364 for tuning).

What this is not

HRSID pixels are finer than free Sentinel-1's 10 m, and the Gulf scenes have no labels, so Gulf accuracy is not measured.

What we did about it

The Gulf passes use a classical CFAR detector that needs no training: 61 px window, 8 sigma, 3 px to 450 m.
